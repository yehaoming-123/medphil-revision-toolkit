# Bioethics Official Requirements

Verified on 2026-07-11 from Wiley's overview and author-guidance pages.

- The journal considers concrete ethical, legal, and policy problems as well as fundamental concepts, principles, and theories.
- The official guidance describes double-anonymized review for original research articles.
- The guidance states that papers over 8,000 words are not normally considered and that abstracts are up to 250 words; recheck these limits before submission.
