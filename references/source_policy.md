# Source Policy

## Allowed sources

- User-provided or authorized manuscripts and notes.
- Official journal and publisher pages.
- Open-access full text.
- PubMed, PubMed Central, Crossref, Europe PMC, DOI metadata, and equivalent legal indexes.

## Evidence labels

- `official_requirement`: Current publisher or journal instruction.
- `observed_tendency`: A pattern summarized from legally accessible recent articles.
- `editorial_inference`: The tool's reasoned editing judgment.
- `abstract_limited`: A conclusion based only on titles, abstracts, or metadata.

Keep facts, corpus observations, and editorial judgments separate in every report.

## Copyright boundary

- Do not bypass paywalls or access controls.
- Do not store copyrighted article full text in the repository.
- Do not copy distinctive wording or imitate a single article.
- Store links, metadata, concise paraphrases, evidence labels, and verification dates.
- Do not publish an author's unpublished manuscript as an example or evaluation fixture.

## Freshness

Recheck official requirements at least every 90 days and immediately before submission. Mark stale or inaccessible sources as unverified rather than silently reusing them.
