# Editorial Safety

## Stop conditions

Stop automated editing and report the reason when:

- The manuscript contains identifiable patient information.
- The file is encrypted, damaged, image-only, or structurally unsafe.
- Citations or references cannot be distinguished reliably from editable prose.
- A requested change would require inventing evidence or an author position.

## Intellectual integrity

- Preserve the author's thesis and argumentative ownership.
- Freeze the reference list by default.
- Preserve names, dates, quotations, citations, DOI strings, and factual claims unless a verified correction is explicitly authorized.
- Do not turn normative claims into empirical findings or examples into evidence.
- Do not strengthen causal, clinical, legal, or policy conclusions beyond available support.

## Author control

Place unresolved conceptual definitions, contested normative commitments, unsupported claims, and meaning-changing edits in `author_confirmation_needed.md`. Keep the original wording when the author must decide.

## Output integrity

Never overwrite the input. Generate a separate clean revision and compare structural counts and citation fingerprints before delivery. Do not claim acceptance probability or AI authorship detection.
